Prayer & candle work

What the colours mean

People ask her this more than almost anything else. Here is how Mother Powers uses them — and she will tell you on the telephone which one your situation actually calls for.

White

Prayer, blessing, peace, truth

The candle Mother lights first, every time, before anything else is done. White carries a prayer and it clears the way. If you only ever burn one candle in your house, burn a white one.

Red

Love, marriage, strength, courage

For love that needs bringing back and for a person who needs their nerve back. Red is a strong colour and Mother does not use it lightly — it moves things quickly.

Pink

Gentle affection, reconciliation, self-love

Where red pushes, pink softens. For a quarrel that needs mending rather than a love that needs pulling back, and for a person learning to think kindly of themselves again.

Green

Money, work, growth, health

Money drawing and steady work. Green is patient — it does not usually come as a windfall, it comes as an offer, a shift, an envelope that turns up.

Purple

Power, wisdom, spiritual opening

Mother's own colour. Purple is for the work that needs authority behind it, and for opening a person up to what they already half know.

Blue

Healing, sleep, calm, protection of the home

For nerves, for grief, for a house where nobody rests. Blue is the quietest candle on the table and the one she reaches for most after white.

Yellow

Clarity, memory, examinations, persuasion

For a decision that will not come clear, for study, and for going in to ask somebody for something you need them to say yes to.

Orange

Doors opening, change, new roads

Road opener work. When every door closes as you reach it, orange is what Mother burns to move the block off the road.

Brown

Court cases, lost things, steadiness

Practical, grounded work. Legal trouble, papers, property lines, and things that have gone missing in a house.

Black

Removal, uncrossing, sending back

Never for harm. Mother burns black to take something off a person — a jinx, a crossing, a condition that is not natural. She will tell you plainly if she thinks black is called for.

A word before you buy candles

You do not need an expensive kit. Mother has watched people spend five and six hundred dollars on packages out of the back of a book and get nowhere, because nobody told them what they were for.

Call her first. She will tell you what your situation calls for, and more often than not it is one candle from the grocery store and a prayer said properly.
