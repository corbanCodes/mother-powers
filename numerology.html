Working the numbers by hand

Mother Powers is a numerologist. She was taught to work numbers off a person's name and birth date, and she has done it the same way ever since.

Your personal year

Every person moves through a nine-year cycle, and where you are standing in it explains a great deal about why a year feels the way it does. Mother works out which year you are in from your birth date, and it is usually the first thing that makes a caller go quiet.

1

Beginnings, standing alone, leadership

A one year is for starting. Bad year to wait for somebody else to move first.

2

Pairs, patience, waiting on somebody

Two asks you to hold your peace. Most of what a two year brings arrives through another person.

3

Word, expression, company

Three is a talking number. Good for asking, telling, selling, and for making up after a fall-out.

4

Work, foundations, the slow build

Four is not exciting and it is not supposed to be. It is the year you fix the roof.

5

Change, travel, the unexpected

Five moves whether you are ready or not. Keep money back in a five year.

6

Home, family, responsibility

Six brings people to your door needing something. It is also the best number for a marriage.

7

Reflection, spirit, going quiet

The most spiritual number and the loneliest. Mother sees a lot of sevens in the people who call her.

8

Money, authority, harvest

Eight is the money number. What you built in the four years comes due here, for better or worse.

9

Endings, letting go, clearing out

Nine finishes things. Do not start anything you want to keep in a nine year — wait for the one.
